Financial information, not personalized advice

The Market Intelligence application provides general market observations, charts, historical numbers, explanatory text and financial education. It does not execute trades or provide individual investment recommendations based on your personal financial situation.

Market Pulse is not a prediction

Market Pulse, confidence, PCR if later introduced, sector colours and AI summaries are software-generated indicators. A positive score does not ensure prices will rise. Confidence is signal agreement, not a win rate or probability of making money.

Data sources and delays

Quotes may come from public or third-party sources, be delayed or cached, and sometimes be unavailable or incorrect. “Checked at” and “data as of” are different timestamps. For any transaction, independently confirm executable prices, product details, costs and risks using your broker or an official exchange-authorized source.

Gold and silver

Gold and silver panels show published India benchmark or indicative figures where available. These are not confirmed individual jeweller counter prices. GST, making charges, purity testing, local premiums and other charges can affect actual buying/selling prices.

AI use

Finance AI may provide an incorrect or incomplete answer or lack access to recent facts. Verify current regulations, taxes, fund data and announcements against primary sources. Never treat a short AI answer as professional financial or legal advice.

Any separately regulated mutual fund distribution or insurance activities are distinct from this educational software and subject to the relevant registrations and disclosures.
