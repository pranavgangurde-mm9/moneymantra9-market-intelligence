What the app does

MoneyMantra 9 Market Intelligence is a mobile-friendly web application for people who want a quick view of Indian market conditions. It combines market indicators, short explanations, historical charts and financial learning tools.

Market Watch

Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, Sensex, India VIX, GIFT Nifty and selected global markets, based on the latest data our sources make available.

Stock Watch

Search supported NSE tickers, switch between timeframes and inspect a stock's available historical closes.

Sector View

Review 12 Indian sector signals, open explanations and select constituent stocks to study them further.

Gold, Silver and AI

Explore dated Indian bullion reference prices and ask short educational questions in the Finance AI tab.

How the app is delivered

The product is provided online as a progressive web app (PWA). It can be opened in a browser and may be installed to the phone's home screen through a supported browser. There is no physical shipment or device included.

Market data comes from external sources and may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. The refresh indicator shows when the app last checked sources, not a guarantee that every underlying quote is real-time.
